# The Vornik logo

This page is where the Vornik logo's proportions and colours are published.
The [trademark policy](https://github.com/EaseIT-cz/vornik/blob/main/TRADEMARKS.md)
lets you use the logo to point at the project at these proportions and
colours.

![The Vornik mark](assets/vornik-mark.svg)

## What the mark is

A pair of square brackets holding three dots: two blue dots over one green
dot. The brackets are the harness that agents work inside. The two blue dots
are the agents. The green dot is the outcome a person approved.

## Colours

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Tile and brackets on light backgrounds | Navy `#101826` |
| Agent dots on light backgrounds | Blue `#1C7ED6` |
| Outcome dot on light backgrounds | Green `#6EA83B` |
| Brackets on navy | White `#FFFFFF` |
| Agent dots on navy | Blue `#6FB4EE` |
| Outcome dot on navy | Green `#9BDB6B` |

A single-colour version (all navy, or all white) is fine where only one
colour is available.

## Rules

- Keep the proportions: do not stretch, rotate or re-space the brackets
  or the dots.
- Do not change, add or reorder the dots, and do not open the brackets.
- Leave clear space of at least one bracket's width on every side.
- Use the mark at 16 px or larger. The wordmark "Vornik" is set in Space
  Grotesk Bold.

## Files

The mark ships with the software: it is the web UI's browser icon and its
app icon on phones. For a logo file in another format, ask
[EaseIT Labs](https://github.com/EaseIT-cz/vornik/issues).
