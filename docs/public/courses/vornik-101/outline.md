# Vornik 101 reading plan

The main deliverable is the [short guide](index.md), targeting **10–15 minutes** of reading. Installation and waiting for a task are outside that reading budget. The guide assumes experience with AI assistants and little familiarity with Vornik.

| Section | Reading budget | Reader takeaway |
|---|---:|---|
| Mental model and diagram | 2 min | Claude interacts; Vornik runs durable background work |
| Connect and verify | 2 min | Endpoint, individual key, plugin, identity and catalog |
| RAG facts | 2 min | Remember, recall, provenance, scopes and correction |
| Workflows | 2 min | Versioned instructions with executable steps; adjust and verify |
| Knowledge skills | 2 min | Reusable procedures, human approval and application |
| Daily loop and team reuse | 2–3 min | Combine the pieces; reproduce with individual credentials |

Total editorial estimate: **12–13 minutes**, with margin up to 15. This has not yet been timed with learners.

The guide uses Community Edition paths. It links to installation documentation and an [optional detailed connection lab](lesson-01.md). Research and verification are supporting author materials, not extra chapters the reader must complete.

Success means the reader can explain the concepts, identify the setup steps, and use the supplied prompts to begin the loop on a provisioned deployment. Full installation, workflow execution, and teammate reproduction need additional practice; they are not promised within the reading budget.

See [requirements](requirements.md) for the user's explicit scope and [research](research.md) for source evidence and remaining verification.
